# Favio Samillan Flores

Economist (PUCP) and research assistant at the Centro de Investigación de la
Universidad del Pacífico (CIUP), Lima.

I work on climate change, migration and forced displacement, with side interests
in rural development and health economics. Mostly household surveys and climate
data from Peru, analysed in R, Python and Stata.

- Portfolio: https://lotuslatte.github.io
- LinkedIn: https://www.linkedin.com/in/f-samillan

## Projects

Prototypes, open to collaboration and funding:

- [Observatorio Laboral del Perú](https://lotuslatte.github.io/peru-labour-observatory/): Peru's labour market from ENAHO microdata, 2014–2024 ([code](https://github.com/lotuslatte/peru-labour-observatory)).
- [Observatorio de Migración del Perú](https://lotuslatte.github.io/peru-migration-observatory/): internal migration, the Venezuelan population and climate mobility ([code](https://github.com/lotuslatte/peru-migration-observatory)).

I also write poetry and essays at *Café sin Azúcar*.
